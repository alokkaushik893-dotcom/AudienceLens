HOW OUR RATINGS WORK

Why a film with 45 votes doesn't outrank one with 2 million

A film with a handful of five-star reviews might look perfect at first glance, but it hasn't stood the test of a wider audience. We balance average ratings against total vote volume so established classics aren't knocked off the list by low-sample flukes. The more people who love a film, the more confident we can be that it's worth your evening.
